The Problem

Earth's orbit is getting crowded

Decades of launches have left tens of thousands of tracked objects — dead satellites, spent rocket stages, and fragments from past collisions — circling the planet at thousands of kilometers per hour. Every one of them is a potential threat to the active satellites we rely on for communication, navigation, and weather forecasting.

What We Built

An end-to-end orbital awareness system

01

Real-Time Tracking

Search a live catalog of ~19,000 satellites and debris, track up to 8 at once, and watch their SGP4-propagated positions update live — or fast-forward up to 5 hours to see where they'll be.

02

Collision Risk Screening

Every tracked satellite is continuously screened against the full catalog for its closest predicted approach over the next 5 hours, scored into Critical/High/Moderate/Low risk bands.

03

3D Orbital Visualization

An interactive globe renders live orbital paths and markers in a way that's intuitive to explore, not just read as a table.

04

Close Approaches & Alerts

Browse every close approach for each tracked satellite, grouped by satellite, plus a standing list of the Critical/High risk approaches across everything you're tracking.
