The Problem

Earth's orbit is getting crowded

Decades of launches have left tens of thousands of tracked objects — dead satellites, spent rocket stages, and fragments from past collisions — circling the planet at thousands of kilometers per hour. Every one of them is a potential threat to the active satellites we rely on for communication, navigation, and weather forecasting.

What We Built

An end-to-end orbital awareness system

01

Real-Time Tracking

Continuously ingest orbital data to plot debris and active satellites as they move through near-Earth space.

02

Collision Risk Prediction

Model close-approach probabilities between tracked objects and surface risk scores before conjunctions occur.

03

3D Orbital Visualization

An interactive globe renders orbital paths and hotspots in a way that's intuitive to explore, not just read as a table.

04

Early-Warning Alerts

Flag high-risk encounters ahead of time, giving operators a window to plan avoidance maneuvers.
